Refund policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Try the free version of any extension first. It shows you how the tool works before you pay. If Pro still isn't right for you, here's how refunds work.

14 days, any plan

  • Monthly: you can get a full refund of your first payment within 14 days. Later monthly renewals are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time so you won't be charged again.
  • Lifetime: you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

You don't need to give a reason. If a renewal charged you by mistake, for example after you tried to cancel, email us and we will sort it out.

How to ask for a refund

Email Devs@oaktreetech.com from the address you used at checkout, with your order number from the receipt. We process refunds through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, and the money returns to your original payment method. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.

After a refund

Your license key is disabled and the extension returns to the free version. Your saved data stays in your browser.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any refund rights you have under the consumer law of your country.
