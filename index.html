Questions

Before you install

Do I need to create an account?

No. The free version works as soon as you install it. Pro only needs the license key from your receipt email.

Does TabForge see what I browse?

No. We don't run servers that collect your data. Your settings, contacts and history stay in your browser. The only data that leaves it is described in each extension's privacy policy, such as a license check or text you send to the AI provider you picked.

Why do SumUp AI and LinkedReach ask for my own AI key?

So you pay the AI provider directly at their price and your text goes straight to them. The key is stored in your browser and sent only to that provider. SumUp AI also works with Chrome's built-in AI, which needs no key.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes. One license key works on up to 3 Chrome profiles or devices. You can free a slot by removing the key from an old device.

How do I cancel a monthly plan?

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your receipt email. Pro stays on until the end of the month you paid for, then the extension returns to the free version. Your data is kept.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment for any plan. See the refund policy.

Who processes my payment?

Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. They handle the payment, sales tax and your invoice. We never see your card details.
